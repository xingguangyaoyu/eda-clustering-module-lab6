One score per node

Transcript

The full narration, one paragraph per slide.

01 · Title
This is step three of the clustering course: spectral bisection. We start with a graph, give every node a score, and turn that score into a cut. Three steps, one tiny graph, no hand-waving.

02 · The problem
Five nodes, six weighted edges, and we want two clusters. Two rules: keep the cut small, and keep the two sides similar in size. A small cut alone is trivial — just isolate one node. That is why the balance rule exists.

03 · Read the weights
Inside A, B and C the edges are five, four and one, so they are tightly bound. D and E are joined by five as well. Between the two groups there is only a weak bridge: two, and one. So the answer is almost visible.

04 · Laplacian
Step one: build the Laplacian. The diagonal holds each node's weighted degree; every edge puts minus its weight off the diagonal. Every row sums to zero, and that is your check.

05 · What it measures
x transpose L x equals the sum over edges of weight times the squared difference of the endpoint values. If two strongly connected nodes get very different values, the cost is high.

06 · Fiedler vector
The smallest eigenvector is flat, so it says nothing. The second smallest is the Fiedler vector. Here E and D come out negative, and C, B, A positive. The sign is already a suggestion.

07 · Sort
E, then D, then C, then B, then A. The graph collapses to one line. Any cut between two neighbours is a candidate, so there are four candidates.

08 · Sweep
Try every balanced prefix. k equals one: cut six. k equals two: cut three. k equals three: cut five. k equals four: cut six. Keep the smallest — the choice is k equals two.

09 · Why not the middle
The middle of the order is not the answer: k equals three scores five, not three. Counting nodes knows nothing about edge weights. Scores propose; the sweep decides.

10 · Result
A, B, C against D and E, cut three. C–D is two, C–E is one, so the two heavy edges never get cut. Same answer as greedy and label propagation, found without merging anything.

11 · Traps
The eigenvector can flip sign; the partition is unchanged. A disconnected graph makes lambda two zero, so check components first. Ties need a stable sort. Next module: Kernighan–Lin refinement.
